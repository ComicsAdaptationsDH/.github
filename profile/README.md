## Comics & Adaptations - Digital Humanities

# Project


**ComicsAdaptDH** is a digital humanities project devoted to the **computational analysis of comics adaptations and rewritings of literary works**. Building on a corpus of contemporary comics in dialogue with Herman Melville’s *Moby-Dick*, the project aims to develop methods for the large-scale study of multimodal narratives combining text and image.

The project involves the creation of a structured corpus of digital comic pages, either obtained from publishers or digitised from print editions. This corpus will be used to experiment with image analysis, OCR, colour analysis, character detection, and multimodal annotation workflows. The objective is both to objectify observations traditionally made through close reading and visual analysis and to explore patterns that become visible only at a larger scale.

By combining comics studies, adaptation studies, and computational methods, ComicsAdaptDH seeks to contribute to the development of reproducible workflows for the analysis of graphic narratives and their cultural transformations.
